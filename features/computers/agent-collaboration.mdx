> Specialized agents across your Brand and Computers coordinate autonomously in the background, sharing context and assets without requiring manual hand-offs.

## What it is

Nutgraf does not rely on a single monolithic assistant or disconnected tools. Instead, each capability operates with specialized multi-agents using a computer. When one agent requires assets, brand styling, or guidelines managed by another agent, they coordinate directly behind the scenes.

For example, when you launch a run in Creative Studio, the video agent does not pause to ask you for your presenter's photograph, voice calibration, or audience guidelines. It coordinates with the Brand agent directly, retrieves the verified assets, and continues production without interruption.

| Collaboration Area | How It Operates |
| :- | :- |
| **Context Sharing** | Computer agents autonomously pull creative direction and voice guidelines from your Brand |
| **Asset Handoff** | Presenter configurations and visual identities are shared directly between agents |
| **Background Execution** | Operational details and asset exchanges happen behind the scenes without user prompts |
| **Boundary Isolation** | Strict isolation ensures agent collaboration never crosses between different Brands |

## How agents collaborate

### 1. Automatic context retrieval

When you launch a Computer run, its agents automatically identify the creative guidelines and assets available in your Brand workspace. You never need to copy-paste rules or repeat instructions between sessions.

### 2. Autonomous asset coordination

If a Computer needs specific brand materials to complete its task — such as digital presenter settings, voice models, or styling rules — it requests them directly from the Brand agent.

### 3. Background coordination

Agents handle operational handoffs and verification behind the scenes. Production continues smoothly while agents resolve context, format guidelines, and styling parameters among themselves.

### 4. Seamless delivery

Once the necessary brand context is applied, the agents finalize the build and deliver the completed first draft to your Stories bar. You only interact with the finished outcome.

## What You Need to Know

### Strict brand isolation

Agent collaboration is permanently scoped to a single Brand workspace. Agents can only coordinate with other agents within that same Brand. Knowledge, assets, and run histories never leak across different Brands or accounts.

### Focus on creative approvals, not operational handoffs

Agents only reach out to you when a genuine creative decision or milestone approval is required — such as confirming an app architecture plan or reviewing a completed video draft. Routine asset coordination happens autonomously.

### Consistent brand voice across all outputs

Because Computer agents always coordinate with the central Brand agent, every video rendered and every app generated adheres faithfully to the master brief and voice you defined.

### Inactive computers wake automatically

When an agent needs information from another part of the workspace, the system coordinates in the background even if that computer is currently idle, ensuring your active session never stalls.

## Tips

- **Keep your Brand brief up to date.** Because all Computer agents pull their guidance from the Brand agent, updating your master brief automatically aligns all future runs.
- **Upload presenter assets during Brand setup.** Setting up your presenter headshot and voice once allows video agents to assemble videos without ever pausing to ask for assets.
- **Trust the background coordination.** You do not need to re-type brand voice or target audience parameters into individual prompts.
- **Look for badges in Stories for approvals.** If an agent genuinely needs your sign-off to proceed, a badge dot will appear on the story to let you know.
