> Creative Studio is the video Computer. Provide a topic or brief, and multi-agents using a computer handle scripting, narration, presenter animation, and rendering — delivering a broadcast-ready first draft in approximately 15 minutes.

## What it is

Creative Studio is the video-building capability inside Nutgraf. Multi-agents using a computer take an approved topic from brief through script, voice synthesis, digital presenter animation, and scene assembly — without you managing a production pipeline. The output is a broadcast-ready first draft delivered in approximately 15 minutes.

Every run operates within your Brand's context. The digital presenter, voice calibration, and creative direction you set in the Brand brief are applied automatically. You focus on approving topics and reviewing the build; the Computer handles the rest.

## Getting Started: Your First Video in Minutes

### 1. Select the Video Computer

Open the input bar inside your Brand and click **+**. Select **Video** from the options. A tooltip confirms what Video does: "Make a short video from a brief: script, narration, scenes and render." The input bar updates to show the Video Computer is active.

### 2. Provide a topic or brief

Type your topic into the input field — for example, "Real AI agent incidents and the defences that stop them." Be specific about the subject and angle. The more directional the brief, the less the agents need to infer. You can also include format guidance: preferred length, tone, or any specific scenes you want included.

### 3. Review the first draft

Multi-agents using a computer produce the first draft in approximately 15 minutes. The completed video appears in your Stories bar as a new session tab. Open it to watch the full draft. The session includes the script, scene breakdown, and a list of the creative choices the agents made.

### 4. Refine through conversation

If a scene needs adjustment, describe the change in the conversation panel. "Make the opening scene more direct — cut the setup and get to the finding in the first ten seconds." The agents revise the specific scene and return the updated draft within the same session. You can iterate on individual scenes without re-rendering the entire video.

## What You Need to Know

### Digital presenters

The digital presenter for a video is drawn from the Brand's presenter configuration. Upload a single photograph in Brand settings to generate the presenter. The voiceprint is calibrated during setup. If no presenter is configured, Creative Studio uses a default presenter until you add one.

### Credit cost per run

Each Video run has a fixed credit price shown before the run starts. The full amount is reserved from your balance before execution begins. The credit cost covers the entire production — scripting, narration, animation, and rendering.

### Scene-level editing

Refinements through conversation target individual scenes, not the full video. This keeps revision fast and preserves scenes you are already happy with. If you want to change the overall direction significantly, starting a new run is usually faster than revising scene by scene.

## Tips

- **Approve a clear topic, not a vague subject.** "AI agent security incidents from 2025 — three real cases and how they were stopped" produces a tighter script than "AI security".
- **Review the scene list before requesting changes.** The session includes a scene-by-scene breakdown that makes it easy to pinpoint which part needs work.
- **Use the Brand brief to handle standing creative decisions.** Tone, format, and audience belong in the brief — not in every individual Video prompt.
- **Request one revision at a time.** Batching multiple scene changes into a single message works, but reviewing one change at a time gives you more control over the final output.

## Frequently Asked Questions

    A complete first draft takes approximately 15 minutes from topic submission to delivery. Refinement requests on individual scenes are faster, typically a few minutes per revision.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use different presenters for different videos under the same Brand?">
    Not currently. Each Brand has one configured presenter that applies to all Video runs within that Brand. Support for multiple presenters per Brand is planned.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to the video file after the run completes?">
    The completed video is stored with the story and remains accessible for as long as the Brand exists. You can download it directly from the session.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I provide my own script instead of letting the agents write one?">
    Yes. Include your script in the topic field or paste it into the conversation. Indicate clearly that it is a finalised script and instruct the agents to proceed directly to production.
  </Accordion>
